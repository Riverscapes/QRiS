Riverscapes Studio (QRiS) is a powerful QGIS plugin for capturing, organizing, analyzing, and sharing riverscape data through time. Built for practitioners, researchers, and analysts, QRiS provides a flexible structure for understanding riverscapes, designing restoration actions, documenting as-built conditions, tracking change, and collaborating across projects and teams.

QRiS supports the full life of a riverscape project—from assessment and planning through design, implementation, monitoring, and analysis—while keeping your data consistent, reusable, and easy to share.
